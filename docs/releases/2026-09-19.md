# Release Notes - 2026-09-19

## Release 2026.9.19

`altium-cruncher` version `2026.9.19` corrects the physical placement and
visibility defects reported for Toon component illustrations. It uses the
complete authored affine placement, region-local finished board thickness, and
Geometer's post-transform half-space clipping. Through-board pins now appear on
both physical sides without duplicating hidden package bodies, bottom-side SMD
bodies no longer leak into top views, and clipped projections use their own
projected bounds so connector pins remain registered to their pads.

Edge, straddle, cutout-overhang, and reverse-mount parts are composed from a
surface-clipped projection over board material and a complete projection through
true open space. The open-space policy is side-aware: board edges, routed
cutouts, NPTH slots, and applicable through bores reveal geometry, while tented,
capped, blind, and buried bores remain opaque on the relevant side.

Rigid-flex rendering now resolves substrate, outer copper, solder mask, and
coverlay per board region. A new flat `BEND_LINES` virtual layer renders authored
bend annotations without folding the board, with configurable color, opacity,
line width, dash/gap lengths, and absolute endpoint extension. Silkscreen may be
left unchanged or clipped to the physical board or resolved film domain.

Toon queues nonfatal missing-model and degenerate-geometry diagnostics, groups
them at completion, and can write a deterministic `toon.warning_report.a0` JSON
report. Components without an authored STEP, extrusion, or supported analytic
body remain intentionally absent; Cruncher does not invent their geometry from
component-height metadata.

The additive PCB SVG/Toon configuration contract advances to
`pcb.svg.config.a1`. Existing A0 files remain accepted, while generated configs,
templates, and resolved output identify as A1. Breaking or replacement config
shapes remain reserved for a future B0 generation.

This release pins the qualified geometry stack exactly:

- `altium-monkey==2026.9.19`
- `wn-geometer==2026.9.19`

The Bluetooth Sentinel rigid-flex board and two approved projection stress
boards are retained as test-only fixtures and are excluded from published
package artifacts. The issue reporter's source file and reference screenshot
are not included.
